Research Proposal

 

Question: What effect has social media had on Democracy in the eyes of American citizens?

Hypothesis: With the uptick in false information and targeted content being so polarizing, we think the popular opinion in America will be that social media has had a negative impact on Democracy in America

Explanatory Variable: Our Explanatory variable will be what country those completing the survey are from. This is measured by having the outcome variable grouped by country.

Outcome Variables: Our outcome variables will be the responses to a questionnaire asking about a variety of things from satisfaction with democracy in your country to internet usage to social media.

The observations and patterns that would support our hypothesis would be for Americans to have a higher percentage of people responding negatively to social media’s effect on Democracy than other countries. These other countries would also need to have comparable internet and social media usage to the US since countries without this are more likely to believe social media does not impact democracy in their country.
